# Recurring Revenue and Break-Even Model

## Status

**Modelled proposal.** This document describes a commercial planning model.
It does not establish measured revenue, profitability, market demand, customer
renewal, or scientific validation.

## Proposed offer

**Spark Holo Marketing-as-a-Service** combines:

- A holographic display rental or customer-supplied display.
- Monthly marketing and content updates.
- Campaign scheduling and remote publishing.
- Accessibility and bilingual content options.
- Optional NFC campaign access.
- Optional approved avatar-service functions.
- Paid disability-led creative, testing, and support roles.

## Recurring revenue

Monthly recurring revenue is the sum of separately contracted services:

```text
R = service + rental + content + accessibility + NFC + avatar + other
```

The proposed base service price is CAD 1,300 per month on a 12-month
agreement. This is a proposed commercial planning input, not a verified market
price or a realized sale.

## Monthly contribution

Monthly contribution is:

```text
M = R - V
```

Where:

- `M` is monthly contribution.
- `R` is monthly recurring revenue.
- `V` is monthly variable delivery cost.

Variable delivery costs may include AI usage, hosting, support hours, content
production, accessibility testing, maintenance, rental recovery, payment
processing, and other direct costs.

## Break-even subscriptions

```text
break-even subscriptions = ceil(monthly fixed costs / average monthly contribution)
```

The calculation requires a positive average monthly contribution. If average
contribution is zero or negative, the model returns no break-even count.

## Rental recovery

Rental pricing should recover more than the equipment purchase price:

```text
rental price =
  (equipment + installation + risk reserve) / service life
  + monthly maintenance
  + required rental margin
```

The model separates rental recovery from creative-service revenue. This
prevents unclear hardware economics and unlimited-service exposure.

## Workforce boundary

Disability-led employment is an operating design, not an automatic outcome.

The model tracks:

- Paid roles.
- Paid hours.
- Hourly compensation.
- Accommodation costs.
- Subsidy support.
- Subsidy end date.
- A funding-independent continuation plan.

Grants, wage supports, and sponsorship revenue must remain separate from
customer subscription revenue.

## Decision rules

| Condition | Required action |
|---|---|
| Average contribution is negative | Redesign price, scope, or delivery cost |
| Fixed costs are unknown | Do not report break-even |
| Rental cost is not separated | Do not offer an all-inclusive subscription |
| Content work is unlimited | Do not offer a fixed monthly price |
| Subsidy covers payroll | Track separately and plan for its end |
| Customer requests custom work | Quote it separately |
| Outdoor installation is proposed | Require engineering, safety, and maintenance review |

## Required evidence before commercial claims

Before describing the service as commercially validated, collect:

- A signed customer agreement or purchase order.
- Supplier quotations for equipment and installation.
- Measured monthly delivery and support costs.
- Documented content workload and revision volume.
- Accessibility-testing results.
- Customer-renewal or cancellation data.
- A cash-flow schedule including payment timing and working capital.
